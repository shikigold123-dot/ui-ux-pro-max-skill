## Rules of thumb
- **The talk is the spine**, the event is the body. Pick lines that each map to a picture you actually have.
  If a metaphor has no matching shot, cut the line rather than illustrate it with something generic.
- **Hook within 2 s.** A chat cold open, a question in huge type, or the best action frame frozen with a word.
- **Tempo map**: under voice 1.4–6 s per shot (people must read); in the action 1–2 beats per shot with occasional
  4-beat holds; climax: few shots, long black. The user explicitly asked for "sometimes fast, sometimes slower".
- **Silence is the strongest effect** — stop the music dead for the hardest line, let only a hall tail ring.
- **Release after the climax**: light (a line of light opening a scene), music resumes, calmer type.
- **End on people** (group/winners photo), title + 2–3 facts (points, record, goals), place · date, fade to black.
- Duration 60–100 s for Instagram/WhatsApp; the opener must earn its seconds.
- Language of all on-screen text = language of the event. Arabic/Islamic phrases in Amiri, correct honorifics
  (ﷺ after the Prophet's name, ﷻ / "subhanahu wa ta'ala" after Allah when spoken).

## Ideas per event type (adapt, don't copy)
- **Football / futsal**: clock ring, tactics board with positions, ball→Earth, scoreline dust, whistle SFX, ABPFIFF.
- **Calisthenics / street workout**: rep counter odometer, freeze-frame on the hold with angle lines/arc measuring the
  body line, bar becomes a horizon line, "SET 3/3" HUD, chalk-dust particles (dustText) on release, heartbeat in the
  hardest hold, slow push-in on the face only if allowed.
- **Boxing / MMA**: round timer ring, punch-count ticker, red accent, impact frames with 1-frame flash + rgbSplit,
  bell SFX instead of whistle.
- **Running / charity run**: distance odometer, route line drawing on a map plate, finish-line portal.
- **Conference / lecture / halaqa**: speaker is the spine; quotes as hero blocks behind the speaker (person mask),
  audience reactions as cutaways, key term in Arabic + translation.
- **Wedding / family**: no HUD, serif-heavy, warm accent, slow pace, music-led; voice = vows or a speech.
- **Community / charity**: numbers (meals, donors, € raised) as odometers, faces only with consent.
